Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our security services and framework.

Do you provide security guards in Mumbai?

Yes, Alpha Seven provides professional security guards across Mumbai including Powai, Hiranandani, Andheri, Ghatkopar, Vikhroli, Thane, Navi Mumbai, BKC, Mulund, and Airoli. All guards are background verified and trained.

Are guards background verified?

Absolutely. Every security guard employed by Alpha Seven undergoes thorough background verification, including police verification, identity checks, and reference validation before deployment.

Do you provide event security?

Yes, Alpha Seven offers comprehensive event security services for private parties, corporate events, concerts, and high-profile gatherings. Our services include crowd management, VIP protection, access control, and emergency planning.

Do you install CCTV systems?

Yes, we provide CCTV installation, burglar alarm systems, centralized monitoring, and remote surveillance solutions as part of our technology-driven security services.

How quickly can deployment begin?

We can begin deployment within 24-48 hours for most residential and corporate security needs. For event security, we require 2-3 days of lead time for comprehensive planning and resource allocation.
